About me

Network & Cybersecurity Specialist

I'm currently pursuing a Diploma in Computer Network and Systems Administration, while expanding my knowledge in cybersecurity and networking through additional training and hands-on practice.

My primary interest is network infrastructure: understanding how networks are designed, configured, managed, secured and troubleshot in real-world environments. Rather than limiting my learning to theory, I focus on practical implementation. I use network simulation and lab environments to design networks, experiment with configurations, troubleshoot failures and document the results.

My current learning path includes networking, systems administration, cybersecurity, Cisco technologies and Fortinet technologies. My goal is to become a dependable networking professional who can support, administer, troubleshoot and secure modern network environments.
